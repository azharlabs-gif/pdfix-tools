Frequently asked questions

Some quick answers about PDFix and its tools.

Is PDFix free to use?

PDFix is designed around free PDF tools. Individual tools may have technical limitations depending on their implementation.

Do I need to install software?

The website is designed to provide PDF tools directly through the browser where possible.

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

This depends on the individual tool. Browser-side processing will be used where technically possible and supported.

Can I use PDFix on my phone?

Yes. The website is being designed to work across desktop, tablet and mobile screens.

How do I find a specific PDF tool?

Use the search box or category filters in the All Tools section to quickly find a tool.
